##  Vrinda Store Data Analysis Summary (2022)

### 1. Objective
Vrinda Store analyzed 2022 sales data to understand customer behavior, product demand, and regional performance. The goal was to identify growth opportunities and shape strategies for 2023.

### 2. Dataset Overview
- **Customer Info**: ID, Gender, Age, Age Group  
- **Order Info**: Order ID, Date, Month, Status, Quantity, Amount  
- **Product Info**: SKU, Category, Size  
- **Channel & Segment**: Channel, B2B  
- **Shipping Info**: City, State, Postal Code  

### 3. Data Preparation
- **Cleaning**: Removed duplicates/nulls, standardized formats  
- **Transformation**: Derived Revenue, Order Month, B2B flag, Age Groups  
- **Aggregation**: Summarized by city, category, channel, and age group  

### 4. Key Findings
- **Monthly Trends**: Sales peaked mid-year; discount periods impacted revenue  
- **Top Cities**: Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Mumbai; Pune and Kolkata emerging  
- **Channel Performance**: Online (esp. Amazon) dominated; Retail/Wholesale underused  
- **Customer Segments**: Age 26–35 most active; 18–25 also engaged  
- **Product Preferences**: Popular sizes: M, L, XL; Top categories: Electronics, Clothing  

### 5. Metrics Snapshot

|------------------------|---------------|
| Total Orders           | 31,047        |
| Total Revenue          | ₹21,176,377   |
| Top State              | Maharashtra   |

| Top Category           | Ethnic Dress  |

| Dominant Channel       | Amazon        |

### 6. Strategic Insights
- Invest further in online channels and mobile platforms  
- Target mid-age customers with loyalty programs  
- Optimize inventory for popular sizes and categories  
- Expand regionally in Pune and Kolkata  

### 7. Recommendations for 2023
- **Channel**: Mobile app, influencer marketing, in-store exclusives  
- **Inventory**: Predictive stocking based on trends  
- **Customer**: Personalized offers, loyalty programs  
- **Regional**: Localized campaigns in high-potential cities  

### 8. Conclusion
Vrinda Store’s 2022 analysis highlights strong digital performance, regional growth potential, and a youthful customer base. Strategic alignment in marketing, inventory, and channel focus will drive success in 2023.
